Delete Your Petrio Account & Data

Last updated: July 26, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Petrio account and the data associated with it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to MLE.Studio@hotmail.com with the subject line "Delete my account".
  2. Include the email address you used to sign in to Petrio, so we can locate your account.
  3. We will confirm and process your deletion request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Requesting account deletion removes, from our cloud database (Firebase):

  • Your account (email address, sign-in credentials)
  • Your trial start date and trial-used status
  • Your subscription status and expiry date

What is not affected

Petrio stores your pets, care tasks, reminders, photos, notebook entries, and weight/walk history only on your own device — this data was never sent to us and is not part of an account deletion request. To remove it, uninstall the app or clear its data from your device's app settings.

If you have an active paid subscription, deleting your account does not automatically cancel or refund it. Manage or cancel subscriptions directly through Google Play (Play Store app > Payments & subscriptions).

Retention

Account data is deleted from our systems within 30 days of a verified request. We do not retain a copy afterward.

Questions

Contact MLE.Studio@hotmail.com for anything else related to your account or this process.
