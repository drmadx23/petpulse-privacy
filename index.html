Petrio Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Petrio ("the app", "we", "us") is a pet care reminder app published by the app's developer. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Data stored only on your device

Most of what you put into Petrio never leaves your phone. This includes:

  • Pet profiles: name, type, weight, age, adoption date, family tree links
  • Care tasks and their reminder schedules
  • Task completion / missed-reminder history
  • Pet photos you add
  • Notebook entries and their optional reminders
  • Weight-tracking log entries
  • Walk-tracking sessions (distance, duration, route) — see the Location section below
  • App preferences (theme, notification settings)

This data is stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. We don't have access to it, and it isn't sent to any server unless you use the manual "Export Backup" feature to share a copy yourself (e.g. to save it or move it to a new device) — that export is initiated and controlled entirely by you via your device's own share sheet.

Account data (only if you sign in)

Signing in is optional and is currently only used to manage a free trial and premium subscription — it does not sync your pets, tasks, or notes (that remains fully on-device for now). If you create an account, we store the following in Google Firebase (Firestore), a cloud database service operated by Google:

  • Your email address (via Firebase Authentication)
  • Your trial start date and whether you've used your free trial
  • Your subscription status and expiry date

This data is used solely to determine whether premium features should be unlocked for your account. We do not use it for marketing, and we do not sell or share it with third parties beyond Google's role as our cloud infrastructure provider (Firebase's own privacy terms apply to their handling of this data: firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

Location data

If you use the GPS walk-tracking feature (dogs only), Petrio accesses your device's precise location only while you have an active walk session running, to calculate distance and duration. This location data is stored locally on your device as part of that walk's record and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

Camera

Petrio requests camera access only when you choose to take a photo of a pet from within the app. Photos are saved to the app's local storage on your device.

Notifications and background operation

Petrio schedules local notifications and (optionally) an on-screen animated overlay to remind you about pet care tasks. To do this reliably, the app may request:

  • Permission to run in the background / be excluded from battery optimization, so reminders aren't delayed or dropped by the system
  • The "Display over other apps" permission, used only to show the optional animated reminder overlay you can enable in Settings — this is off by default and never used to display ads or unrelated content

None of this involves sending data anywhere — it's purely local scheduling on your device.

In-app purchases

Subscriptions are purchased through Google Play Billing. We don't see or store your payment details — that's handled entirely by Google Play. We only receive confirmation that a purchase succeeded, which we use to unlock premium features on your account as described above.

Data deletion

Since most of your data lives only on your device, uninstalling the app removes it. If you've signed in, you can request deletion of your account data (email, trial/subscription records) by contacting us at the address below.

Children's privacy

Petrio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. If changes are significant, we'll aim to surface that in the app itself.
